Skinning: which mobs you can skin

Skill needed is 5 × mob level up to level 20, then 10 × level − 100. At skinning the Hunter can skin mobs up to level . Leather tier follows mob level: Light (1–20), Medium (15–32), Heavy (30–42), Thick (40–52), Rugged (50+).
